## Competitors

- Respondent
- G2 Marketing Solutions
- Qualtrics Strategy & Research

Listen Labs operates in the AI-powered qualitative market research sector, positioning itself as an end-to-end platform that automates the entire research workflow—including study design, participant recruitment (from its own 30M+ network), AI-moderated interviews, and automated insight generation (reports, decks, and highlight reels). Its primary value proposition is speed and scale, delivering findings in hours rather than weeks. While it excels at high-volume AI-moderated qualitative research, the competitive landscape includes broader user research platforms that offer a mix of AI and human-led methodologies. Some competitors argue that Listen Labs' focus on AI-moderated interviews alone may lack the methodological diversity required for complex UX research (such as card sorting, tree testing, or unmoderated tasks) that is available in more comprehensive, multi-method research platforms.
